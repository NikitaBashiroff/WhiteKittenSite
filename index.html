Our projects

White Kitten Offline

An ODC (Offline Digital Content) site that can work in places that doesn't have any access to the Internet. Useful for cars, buses, trains, airlines and houses while there isn't any access to the Internet. Offline can be shared through the Wi-Fi access point. Right now it's only available in Russian, but we're working on other languages too.
